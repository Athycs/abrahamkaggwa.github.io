About

I am a public health and medical big data researcher with training in human nutrition, epidemiology, biomedical informatics, and population health. My work has examined how combinations of lifestyle behaviours and existing health conditions relate to mortality and years of life lost.

I am interested in using longitudinal health data and quantitative methods to understand differences in health trajectories, particularly in healthy ageing, multimorbidity, and socioeconomic inequalities. I also study how artificial intelligence and digital tools can be evaluated for safe and equitable use in healthcare.

My experience includes research with the Korean Genome and Epidemiology Study and public health programme implementation in Uganda.

Research interests

Healthy ageing and multimorbidity

How behavioural, clinical, and socioeconomic factors shape functional health, longevity, and resilience across the life course.

Lifestyle epidemiology and survival analysis

Studying combinations of smoking, alcohol use, diet, physical activity, and sleep in relation to mortality and years of life lost.

Health inequalities

Understanding socioeconomic differences in health outcomes and opportunities for prevention.

Digital health and AI evaluation

Evaluating the implementation, safety, quality, and equity of AI-supported triage and patient navigation tools.

Cancer risk and early detection

Exploring how longitudinal symptoms, clinical history, and other routinely collected data may support earlier risk assessment and investigation.

Selected research projects

Lifestyle, morbidity, and mortality in the Health Examinee cohort

Korean Genome and Epidemiology Study (KoGES-HEXA)

Analysed data from more than 111,000 adults to investigate whether the association between combined lifestyle behaviours and mortality differs according to baseline morbidity status.

AI triage systems in primary care

Systematic review

Reviewing evidence on real-world performance, care quality, and implementation factors for AI-enabled triage systems in primary and first-contact care.

Community nutrition and public health

Uganda

Worked on nutrition programming and contributed to district-level nutrition action planning.
